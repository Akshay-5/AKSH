## about


      

        

          

            I'm Akshay, a cybersecurity student working my way toward a
            blue-team role. Most of my time goes into hands-on practice: standing up lab
            environments, tearing apart captured traffic, chasing alerts in a SIEM, and
            working through CTF rooms until the concepts actually stick.
          

          

            I'm interested in the whole picture — how an attacker moves through a network
            and how a defender spots them. That's why I spend as much time
            studying detection engineering and incident response as I do practising
            offensive techniques.
          

          

            Right now I'm working toward my CEH, sharpening my Python and
            Linux skills, and documenting everything I learn along the way.
